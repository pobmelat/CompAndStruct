Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, ``How Composition and Cluster Structure Influence CO Adsorption in \ce{Pt} and Pt--Ge Clusters,'' for publication as an Original Research Article in the \textit{Journal of Cluster Science}.

Earlier studies showed that adding Ge to Pt clusters can weaken CO binding. However, the lowest-energy clusters also change shape as their composition changes. To examine the role of structure, we started from the reported global minima of \ce{Pt10} and \ce{Pt5Ge5}, exchanged selected Pt atoms for Ge or all Ge atoms for Pt, and optimized the resulting clusters. This gives us two \ce{Pt10} isomers that we can compare at the same spin multiplicity. Even with composition and spin held fixed, their CO binding energies differ by about 0.30~eV on average across the Pt sites.

In the \ce{Pt5Ge5} derivative, the frozen-fragment interaction favors one Pt site, yet CO binds 0.42~eV more strongly at the other. The energy decomposition shows that metal-cluster reorganization reverses the site preference.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration by another journal.

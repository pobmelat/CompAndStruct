and cluster structure affect \ce{CO} binding.} Within the Pt--Ge derivative series, the mean $BE_{\rm ad}(\ce{CO})$ becomes less negative as the Ge content increases, although the binding energies vary between Pt sites. AdNDP describes delocalized multicenter bonding in \ce{Pt10^{der}}, even though this isomer was generated from the \ce{Pt5Ge5^{GM}} structure. The Pt--Ge derivatives instead show mainly localized one- and two-center bonding.

The comparison of the two \ce{Pt10} quintet series shows the effect of
structure at fixed composition and multiplicity. Their mean binding energies
differ by about 0.30 eV, with stronger mean binding for \ce{Pt10^{der}}.
\textcolor{black}{\sout{The GM-based Pt--Ge calculations also show that different
isomers of the same composition can give different \ce{CO} binding energies.}}\textcolor{ForestGreen}{The Pt--Ge GM and derivative structures also give different \ce{CO} binding
energies. For \ce{Pt6Ge4}, this comparison includes a change in multiplicity.}

The initial ECoN descriptors distinguish the coordination and local Pt/Ge composition of 
the adsorption sites. Sites with a larger initial Ge fraction tend to bind \(\ce{CO}\) less strongly.
\textcolor{black}{\sout{Sites 6/8 and 7/10 of \ce{Pt5Ge5^{der}CO} have almost
identical initial Ge fractions and similar optimized \ce{Pt-C} distances
and BCP densities, but their binding energies differ by 0.42 eV.
The fragment decomposition shows that this difference is mainly
associated with metal-cluster reorganization.}}\textcolor{ForestGreen}{The comparison between sites 6/8 and 7/10 of \ce{Pt5Ge5^{der}CO} illustrates
why local descriptors need to be considered together with cluster
reorganization. These sites have almost identical initial Ge fractions and
similar optimized \ce{Pt-C} distances and BCP densities, but their binding
energies differ by 0.42 eV. The fragment decomposition relates this difference
mainly to metal-cluster reorganization.}

Changes in ECoN describe local rearrangement, while the reorganization energies measure its energy cost or stabilization. 
\textcolor{black}{\sout{The fragment analysis shows that the relative contributions of interaction and metal reorganization vary between sites and compositions.
Thus, \(BE_{\rm ad}(\ce{CO})\) reflects their combined energetic effects,
relative to the selected bare-cluster reference. Local descriptors and fragment energies help us 
understand the origin of these binding-energy differences.}}\textcolor{ForestGreen}{Across the selected sites and compositions, differences in

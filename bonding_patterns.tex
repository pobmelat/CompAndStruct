\subsection{Bonding patterns in Pt and Pt--Ge clusters}

The AdNDP analysis was used to distinguish the bonding patterns of the Pt and Pt--Ge
clusters. For triplet \ce{Pt10^{der}}, the alpha and beta density matrices were
analyzed separately. The combined description contains three Pt-centered
1c--2e elements per Pt atom, 19 3c--2e elements, and two additional
3c--1e elements from the alpha density
(Supporting Table~\ref{tab:tfg_adndp_summary}).
This describes delocalized multicenter bonding, in agreement with the
earlier AdNDP analysis of high-spin
\ce{Pt10^{GM}}.\cite{ugartemendia:2022} By contrast, \ce{Pt5Ge5^{der}} shows localized bonding, with 20 Pt-centered
1c--2e elements, 14 Pt--Ge 2c--2e bonds, and three Pt--Pt 2c--2e bonds
(Supporting Table~\ref{tab:tfg_adndp_summary}), in agreement with the
localized bonding described for \ce{Pt5Ge5^{GM}}.\cite{ugartemendia:2022}
The \ce{Pt6Ge4} and \ce{Pt4Ge6} derivative models also show
mainly one- and two-center elements. Figure~\ref{fig:tfg_adndp_comparison}
shows selected examples: one multicenter element from \ce{Pt10^{der}} and two
localized bonds from \ce{Pt5Ge5^{der}}. The AdNDP decompositions for all
derivative compositions are summarized in Supporting
Table~\ref{tab:tfg_adndp_summary}.

\subsection{Bonding patterns in Pt and Pt--Ge clusters}

Earlier AdNDP analyses described delocalized multicenter bonding in
\ce{Pt10^{GM}}, while \ce{Pt5Ge5^{GM}} was described by localized
Pt-centered and Pt--Ge bonding.\cite{ugartemendia:2022} We examined whether
these patterns also appear in the derivative structures.

For triplet \ce{Pt10^{der}}, the separate $\alpha$ and $\beta$ density
matrices give three Pt-centered 1c elements per Pt atom in each spin component, 19 paired 3c--2e
elements, and two additional 3c--1e elements in the $\alpha$ density.
This description retains the
delocalized multicenter character of Pt bonding. In
contrast, \ce{Pt5Ge5^{der}} shows localized one- and two-center bonding,
including Pt--Ge and Pt--Pt elements, consistent with the reported description
of \ce{Pt5Ge5^{GM}}. The \ce{Pt6Ge4^{der}} and \ce{Pt4Ge6^{der}} structures
also show localized one- and two-center elements. Element counts and ON ranges
are summarized in Supporting Table~\ref{tab:tfg_adndp_summary}; reported ON
values for the GM structures are listed in the Supporting Information.
Figure~\ref{fig:tfg_adndp_comparison} shows one multicenter element from
\ce{Pt10^{der}} and two localized bonds from \ce{Pt5Ge5^{der}}.
